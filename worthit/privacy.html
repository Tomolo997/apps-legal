Worth It? Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

The short version

Worth It? does not collect any personal data. We have no accounts, no servers that receive your data, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Camera

The camera is used only to read prices. Images are processed on your iPhone in real time with Apple's on-device text recognition. They are never saved, uploaded or shared.

Information stored on your device

Your pay settings (amount, pay period, hours and tax rate), your answers in the intro questions and your "Skipped" history are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Photos

If you choose a photo to scan, it is read on your device and not stored or uploaded. The app only sees the photo you pick.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
