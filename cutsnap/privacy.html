Cutsnap Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026 · applies to the Cutsnap iPhone app

The short version

Cutsnap edits your videos entirely on your iPhone. Your videos, your voice and the transcript of what you say never leave your device. We don't run a server for the app, don't collect personal data, don't show ads and don't track you.

Your videos

Videos you record or import are stored inside the app on your iPhone. Auto Cut, captions, retake detection and export all run on the device, using Apple's on-device speech recognition and, on supported iPhones, Apple Intelligence's on-device model. Nothing is uploaded by Cutsnap.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: only to record videos in the app.
  • Speech recognition: to turn speech into captions and find pauses and retakes. On iPhones that can't recognise speech on the device, Apple's speech service may process the audio under Apple's privacy policy; Cutsnap itself never receives it.
  • Photos: to import the videos you pick and to save the ones you export.
  • Notifications: only if you schedule a batch, to remind you when it's time.

Music you add

Audio files you add as background music are copied into the app on your device. Only use music you have the right to use.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The app checks your subscription with Apple on the device.

Sharing

When you share a finished video to another app (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube…), that app's own privacy policy applies to what you post.

Deleting your data

Delete a video from the Cuts tab, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. An App Store subscription is billed by Apple and must be cancelled in your Apple Account settings.

Children

Cutsnap is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
