Cutsnap Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026 · applies to the Cutsnap iPhone app

The short version

Your videos and your voice stay on your iPhone. To edit a video with AI, Cutsnap sends only the words you said (the transcript, with word timings) to our server, which asks Anthropic's Claude which words to cut. We don't store the transcript. The app has no sign-in, collects no name or email, shows no ads and doesn't track you.

Your videos

Videos you record or import are stored inside the app on your iPhone. Recording, transcription (Apple's on-device speech recognition), pause detection, captions and export all run on the device. Your video and audio are never uploaded by Cutsnap.

AI editing

When you tap Auto Cut, the transcript is sent over an encrypted connection to our server (luxsnapai.com) and on to Anthropic, which provides the Claude AI model, only to decide the edit: which words are retakes, stumbles, filler words or rambling, plus a suggested title and on-screen hook. We keep a count of AI edits and their length for your plan's monthly allowance, but not the words. Anthropic processes the transcript under its commercial terms: it is not used to train its models and is deleted after a short retention period. If AI editing isn't available, Cutsnap edits the video on your iPhone instead.

Your anonymous account

On first launch the app creates an anonymous account on our server, identified only by a random ID that your iPhone keeps. Your plan and your monthly AI-edit count belong to it.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: only to record videos in the app.
  • Speech recognition: to turn speech into captions and the transcript. On iPhones that can't recognise speech on the device, Apple's speech service may process the audio under Apple's privacy policy; Cutsnap itself never receives the audio.
  • Photos: to import the videos you pick and to save the ones you export.
  • Notifications: only if you schedule a batch, to remind you when it's time.

Music you add

Audio files you add as background music are copied into the app on your device. Only use music you have the right to use.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. To unlock your plan, the app sends our server Apple's signed record of the subscription (product, dates and transaction IDs), which we check against Apple's signature and keep with your anonymous account.

Sharing

When you share a finished video to another app (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube…), that app's own privacy policy applies to what you post.

Deleting your data

Delete a video from the Cuts tab, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your iPhone. To delete your anonymous account's plan record and edit counts from our server, email us from the app's support page. An App Store subscription is billed by Apple and must be cancelled in your Apple Account settings.

Children

Cutsnap is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
