How Auto Cut works

Record or import a video and tap Auto Cut. Cutsnap listens to the sound and removes pauses, filler words like "um" and "uh", and sentences you started again. Everything happens on your iPhone. Open Cuts in the editor to see every cut and turn any of them back on.

Plans and AI edits

One Auto Cut on one video is one AI edit. Starter includes 50 a month, Pro 200, and Unlimited is for heavy use (fair use, 1,000 a month). The 3-day free trial includes 10. Re-exporting, changing captions or restoring cuts is free. Your first Auto Cut is free without a plan.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions renew monthly or yearly until cancelled. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in the app: You → Manage subscription.

Restore purchases

Open You → Restore purchases, or Restore on the plans screen, on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account.

Captions are missing

Captions need speech recognition. Allow it in iPhone Settings → Cutsnap, and keep the phone online the first time so iOS can download the language model. Pauses are still cut without it.

Batch editing

Add up to 10 videos in the Batch tab and tap Start now, or pick a time. At that time you'll get a notification, and the batch runs while Cutsnap is open.
