How credits work

One credit makes one AI photo. Plus gives you 5 credits a day, Gold gives you 10. Credits refresh every day at midnight UTC and don't roll over. If a photo fails, its credit comes back automatically.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions bought in the app renew weekly until cancelled. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the week in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in the app: Settings → Manage subscription.

Restore purchases

Sign in with the same account, then open Settings → Restore purchases.

My photo didn't work

Try a clear, well-lit photo with your face visible. Failed photos give their credit back.

Delete your account

Settings → Delete my account removes your account and every photo. See the Privacy Policy.
