LuxSnap AI Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026 · applies to the LuxSnap AI iPhone app

The short version

The free filters run entirely on your iPhone and never upload your photo. AI effects and templates upload only the photo you choose, to make that one edit. Your photos are private to your account and deleted after 30 days. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across apps.

Free filters and Beautify

Film filters, adjustments and Beautify are processed on your device. Beautify finds faces with Apple's on-device Vision framework. These photos are not uploaded, stored by us or shared.

No sign-in

The app has no login and never asks for your name, email or phone number. On first launch it creates an anonymous account on our server that only your device can use, identified by a random ID. Your daily credits, your AI photos and your plan belong to that anonymous account.

AI photos

When you create an AI photo, the photo you picked is uploaded to our servers and sent to our AI provider, fal.ai, only to produce your edit. The original and the result are stored privately (Cloudflare R2) so you can see them in your Gallery, and are deleted automatically after 30 days, or immediately when you delete the photo or your account. AI effects are instructed to change only light and colour; templates recreate a scene using your likeness.

Purchases

Subscriptions in the app are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To unlock your plan, the app sends us Apple's signed transaction record (product, purchase and renewal dates, transaction IDs), and Apple notifies our server of renewals, refunds and cancellations. If you reinstall the app, Restore purchases moves your plan, and the AI photos still kept, to the new anonymous account.

Usage analytics

The app records anonymous product events (for example "paywall viewed" or "photo saved") with PostHog to understand which features are used. These events are not linked to your account or your photos and are not used for advertising.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Delete my photos and data. This deletes your anonymous account, every AI photo and your history from our servers. Deleting the app also stops anything new from being collected; photos left on our servers are deleted automatically after 30 days. An App Store subscription is billed by Apple and must be cancelled in your Apple Account settings.

Children

LuxSnap AI is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
