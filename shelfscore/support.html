Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Shelf Score. You can also manage subscriptions here. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

I already subscribed but the app is locked.
Open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The camera doesn't read well.
Use good light, hold steady and get a little closer. You can also pick a photo instead.
