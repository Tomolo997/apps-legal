Shelf Score Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

The short version

Shelf Score does not collect personal data and does not track you. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

Camera

The camera is used only to read book spines. Images are processed on your iPhone with Apple's on-device text recognition. They are never saved or uploaded.

Book lookups

To show ratings, the app sends the text it read from a spine (for example a title and author) to Open Library, a free book database run by the non-profit Internet Archive, and loads cover images from it. These requests contain no account, name or device identifier from us. Open Library's handling of requests is covered by the Internet Archive's policies.

Information stored on your device

Your "Want to read" list and your answers in the intro questions are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Photos

If you choose a photo of a shelf, it is read on your device and not stored or uploaded. The app only sees the photo you pick.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
